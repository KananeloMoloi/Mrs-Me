MEDIA
=====
assets/images/  the 7 photos (listed, with captions, in js/data.js)
assets/videos/  moment-1.mp4, moment-2.mp4, moment-3.mp4 (used in index.html)

To swap or add a photo: drop the file in assets/images/ and edit js/data.js.
To swap a video: replace the file, or change src="..." on its <video> tag in index.html.
